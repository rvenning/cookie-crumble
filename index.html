❓ How to Play

Everything is a tap. Work up the screen: bench at the bottom, customers at the top.

  1. 🥣 Tap the bench to mix a bowl of dough.
  2. 🍪 Tap a cutter to roll it out and cut the shape a customer asked for.
  3. 🔥 Tap an oven shelf to put the tray in.
  4. ⏲️ Watch the gauge. Take it out on the green stripe and it's perfect. Amber is a bit crispy. After that it's burnt.
  5. 🎨 Tap the icing table, then a colour — and sprinkles, if they asked.
  6. 🧑 Tap the customer whose card matches. They pay you!

Your hands hold one tray. Park a tray on the 🧊 cooling rack when you need a free hand for the oven. Anything you don't want goes in the 🗑️ bin — nothing is ever wasted by mistake.

Stars: one for finishing, two if nobody gives up waiting, three if most of them are baked just right.
