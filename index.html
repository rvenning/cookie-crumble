❓ How to Play

Everything is one tap. You walk there and do it — and the walking is the only thing that costs you.

  1. 🚪 Tap a guest at the door, then a free table. Matching the tablecloth to their colour pays a bonus.
  2. ❓ Tap a table showing ? to take the order.
  3. 🫖 Tap the benches at the bottom — the urn, the oven, the icing and (later on) the sandwich counter. They cook while you're away.
  4. 🍽️ Tap the pass to load your tray with whatever's ready.
  5. 🍰 Tap the table it belongs to. Bring a whole order in one trip for extra points.
  6. 🪙 When they've finished, tap the table again — they pay up as you clear it.

Hearts show how long somebody will wait. Do the same job twice in a row for a chain bonus. Anything you don't want goes in the 🗑️ bin — nothing is ever lost by mistake.

Stars come from your score. The bar along the top shows how close you are.
